# Fantasy NPC reference

The company's coworkers and future enemies include elves, fairies, goblins and orcs in the current Unreal FlipBooks branch. These are provisional Doom-style 2D prototypes. Use the sprites to recognize the fantasy species and to inform symbols and concepts. Character redesign and final appearance remain open.

- Unreal runtime art: `/Game/Programming/Enemies/FlipBooks`
- Editable owner sources: `D:/GameJam/Total (in)Sanitation/Characters`

The eight Unreal folders contain 4,105 packages on disk, including frame textures, sprites and flipbooks. The current `/Game/Art/Characters` destination remains for future work.
