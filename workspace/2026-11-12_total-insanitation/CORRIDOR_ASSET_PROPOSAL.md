# Basement corridor dressing proposal

**12 November 2026 · Owner-accepted direction for modeling · Not implemented in Unreal.**

The owner liked A/B/C and requested a visible OpenCode worker using **DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 (Max)**, supervised and independently reviewed by the current agent. The **Agent production** tab and `projects/total-insanitation/CORRIDOR_ASSET_HANDOFF.md` contain the prepared task. Worker launch is not yet confirmed; this approval covers the direction, not unseen meshes.

Four new native gameplay captures were taken during a continuous PIE exploration of the junction, service corridor, stair and equipment shop. Three were edited with the built-in image generator. The concepts retain the existing layout and use the 1970s pharmaceutical-company service layer to give the empty walls a purpose. The existing shop spikes and a few pointed iron brackets carry the medieval accent.

Open **Basement → Corridor concepts** for the full-size images, downloads and native comparisons. The table below identifies every proposed addition. **N** means new production work, **R** means reuse, and **V** means a material variant. Direction acceptance does not imply that any mesh or engine implementation has been verified.

## Fit to the existing modular kit

- Keep the existing **400 × 400 cm** wall/floor/ceiling convention, **15 cm** wall thickness, edge pivots and **385 cm** shortened wall-corner convention. Read `projects/total-insanitation/MODULE_CONTRACT.md` before authoring.
- Build new pipe/conduit assemblies on **400 cm endpoint spacing**, plus matching **385 cm corner-wall assemblies** and **100 cm infill units** where needed. Coupler and elbow allowances belong inside that assembly footprint; a 400 cm cylinder plus external fittings must not silently grow past the grid. Check pipe centerline offsets at inside and outside corners. The image is not a measured fitting layout.
- Put regular decoration pivots at the owning module edge. Fit the shorter corner assemblies to the current corners, avoid overlapping parallel wall faces and keep collars/brackets from duplicating at seams.
- Keep service runs high on the wall, with a target bottom clearance above **260 cm**, clear of existing lights. Cabinet projection is at most **18 cm**, hose reel **25 cm**, shelf about **30 cm**. These are proposed limits for the first mesh pass, not collision-tested dimensions.
- Add fixtures to existing wall faces. No new doorway, recess, ceiling opening, drain cut or floor rebuild is part of this pass. The route, stair landing, shop serving window and spike delivery opening stay clear. Place props against side walls.
- Preserve existing floor/ceiling UV and tileable-density exceptions. Target the owner's nominal **256 px/m** for applicable modular structures and **512 px/m** for simple props; do not normalize existing UVs to enforce those values.
- Structures and building-service module kits belong inside the existing **`Assets/Building/Interior_Building.blend`**. Individual cabinets, boards, reel and containers belong inside **`Assets/Enviroment_Assets/Enviroment_Assets.blend`**. New sign variants tied to the shop/building stay in the building file. No new blend file is required.
- Future Unreal delivery remains under **`/Game/Art`** with materials assigned in the modular/prop Blueprints. Use the owner's **Super Duper Batch Exporter / Unreal_Only_FBX** configuration, then check the first delivered module in the engine. Do not change shared parents for this proposal.

## Suggested production order

1. **N01 + N02 + V01:** one four-meter wall/ceiling bay and one shortened corner in LaCarlota. Check endpoints, corner fit, normal player clearance and retained light coverage.
2. **N03 + N04 + N05 + T01:** the junction landmark. Check sign direction and text readability at the real gameplay FOV before filling other walls.
3. **N06 + N07 + R01 + R02:** one hose station and the shop supply station. Check the stair, door swing/interaction space and both counter approaches in PIE.

The aim is purposeful repetition with a few landmarks. Do not duplicate every cabinet, sign or board onto every module. Exact word choices and final aesthetic acceptance remain with the owner; no NPC identity or gameplay behavior is proposed here.
