Revise the first draft into a convincingly circa-1974 pharmaceutical-company staff poster, flat print artwork in a straight-on frontal view. Keep the wizard's exact waving faceless silhouette, hat, hands, long staff and round star orb, whole figure visible. Make this more unmistakably 1970s through bold tight geometric sans-serif typography, broad asymmetric stepped stripes and a large flat circular field, with intentional heavyweight print hierarchy. Input 1 is the edit target and controls the mopping wizard silhouette and existing safety wording. Input 2 controls the late-1970s utilitarian public sign family, especially the cream and dark bottle green and the confident sans-serif. Do not include its arrow, destination name or pointing pose.
Redesign the first as a clean, period-credible 1970s custodial instruction placard. Wrap the second text into short lines; keep it all visible. Flat artwork seen dead-on, landscape rectangle centered on a very narrow, plain neutral surround. No wall, perspective, screws, cast shadows, heavy distress, room, mounting, extra instructions, arrows, logos, or other visual context. One polished raster concept for owner review.
