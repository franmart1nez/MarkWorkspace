# Wizard mascot v01 — proposed master

Owner approval pending. These are manually authored editable SVGs and deterministic PNG exports, not approved production assets. The original six wizard JPGs were preserved. No generation or native import was performed.

Open wizard-mascot-v01.svg for the full greeting master, or wizard-mascot-v01-compact.svg for the small-size treatment. Named groups/symbols and transparent masks keep the head, hat, limbs, staff and hands editable. Black/white PNG exports at 512 and 1024 retain identical alpha. Text in the application SVG remains editable and uses Arial with generic sans-serif fallbacks; typography/copy are provisional.

Master-sheet-v01, applications-v01, readability-v01 and context-v01 are review sheets. Three study SVGs demonstrate pointing, mopping and slipping. The twelve-pose batch has not begun.

Proposed rules: H=110-unit head diameter; brim=178, torso=70, arms=32, legs=41 and staff shaft=14 units. Leave at least 55 units clear space. Full minimum: 96 px square canvas. Compact minimum: 64 px square canvas; smaller examples are diagnostic. Staff may switch support hands or be omitted for two-hand work/falling, subject to approval. Do not mirror the hat.

Preserve these v01 sources before hand editing. The project authoring script targets this draft branch; later revisions should use v02 etc. No Unreal asset, atlas or runtime/localization binding was changed. Native-context sheets are pairings, not in-scene mockups.
