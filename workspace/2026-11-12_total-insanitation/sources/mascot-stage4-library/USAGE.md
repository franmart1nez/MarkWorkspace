# Using the wizard mascot

Use the welcome pose for a neutral greeting and the raised hand for a wave. The pointing poses indicate direction. Mopping, pushing equipment and carrying supplies use both hands and omit the staff. The warning poses use pose and hazard symbols for quick recognition. The fire escape retains the staff.

Use black ink on light or yellow surfaces, and white ink on dark surfaces. The SVG cutouts are transparent. Use the SVG for scalable work and export the supplied PNG that matches the target canvas. The master canvas is 1254 × 1254 and retains each selected image’s framing and ground stroke. Scale uniformly. Preserve the hat, orb and grip openings when placing the artwork.

Check the 64, 96 and 128 px samples for hand, tool and lightning detail. At 32–48 px fine openings may close. Evaluate the intended sign distance and Unreal mip behavior in context before production. The background examples are contrast studies for the mascot.
