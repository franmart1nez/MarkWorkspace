# Audit · 2026-11-18

**In short:** the game works. All 31 tests pass, a simulated year takes about
5 seconds, and the people simulation is rich and stable. Its limits are in what
lies under it: the land is a fixed, flat region built around the city;
animals are herds without needs; walking ignores buildings; time rules are tied
to the 48-day year. The documentation had grown in layers across five versions
in two days and mixed in plans from an earlier, abandoned goal. It was
replaced by four short tabs, a roadmap and a technical guide for AI agents.

## What was checked

| Area | How |
| --- | --- |
| Code | All 18 engine modules and 9 viewer files read or searched (about 6,800 lines, plus 700 lines of tests and scripts) |
| Tests | `npm test`: **31 of 31 pass** in 21 seconds |
| Speed | One 48-day year headless on seed `dreams`: **about 5.0 s**; population 1,799 → 1,828; save 1.96 MB |
| Content | Seed `dreams`: 706 buildings, 894 road points, 306 fields, about 58,000 trees, 1,799 people, 408 animals in 70 herds |
| Walking | Walkers sampled every 10 minutes for a day (3,669 samples) and tested against building outlines |
| Viewer | Five fresh captures from the running game; no page errors |
| Documentation | Every Markdown file, card and registry entry of the project |

## The game today

Strong: the people simulation (timetables, interiors, a closed economy that
always balances, trades, lives, illness, crime and corruption), determinism (the
same seed gives the same history, saves continue identically), and the painted
viewer with its observation tools. These are the base of roadmap M4.

## Problems found in the code

| # | Finding | Where | Roadmap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **The land is built around the city.** The river is drawn through a fixed point beside the city at the map centre; villages sit at fixed offsets; the region is always 5,200 × 3,600 m and flat | `src/engine/layout.js` lines 10, 41–68, 482 | M1, M4 |
| 2 | **Time rules are tied to the 48-day year.** Calendar lengths are constants used in ten engine modules; crop growth divides by a literal 48; yearly chances are divided by the year length | `core.js:6`, `sim.js:475`, `sim.js:632–645` | M1.2, M2.4, M5.2 |
| 3 | **Animals ignore the seasonal sun.** People follow sunrise and sunset; wolves and grazers use fixed night hours (19:00 or 20:00 to 05:00) | `wild.js:63`, `wild.js:114` | M1.2.7 |
| 4 | **Walking ignores buildings.** About 0.6% of walking positions (23 of 3,669) were inside a building the person was not entering — straight stretches to and from places off the road. Indoors, people jump from the door to their spot. Nobody steps aside | `sim.js` `start`, `walk`, `arrive`; `interior.js` `seat` | M5.1 |
| 5 | **Animals are herds, not individuals.** Individual animals have no age, sex, hunger or thirst; a herd checks only that its next centre is open ground | `wild.js` | M1.7 |
| 6 | **Two large hub files.** `sim.js` (699 lines) calls every system directly; `layout.js` is one 760-line function. Systems cannot be swapped or tested alone | `sim.js`, `layout.js` | M1.1, M5.5 |
| 7 | **The engine shares the browser's main thread.** Heavy speeds compete with drawing | `src/ui/main.js` | M5.3 |
| 8 | **Old saves are refused, not upgraded.** Each version change makes earlier saves unusable | `index.js` `deserialize` | M5.4.2 |
| 9 | **Node and browser determinism is not tested.** Tests compare runs within one runtime | `test/` | M5.4.4 |
| 10 | **Six species live in the population.** The roadmap's first kingdom is humans only | `people.js` `SPECIES_MIX` | M4.3.6 |
