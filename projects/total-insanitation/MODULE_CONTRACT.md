# Modular kit conventions

Retained production conventions for the owner's existing kit. The [stage-2 file contract](../../workspace/2026-11-12_total-insanitation/FILE_PLACEMENT.md) records current source/export/package relationships and missing-export exceptions. Existing validation packages are still referenced by `LaCarlota`; preserve them without treating them as new production deliverables.

| Piece | Convention |
| --- | --- |
| Grid | 400 cm |
| Regular wall | 400 cm long, 400 cm high, 15 cm thick |
| Short corner wall | 385 cm long, same height/thickness |
| Floor | 400 × 400 cm, 15 cm thick; walking surface at pivot height |
| Decorations | Match the 4 m span, with shorter corner variants |

Keep edge pivots and intentional floor/ceiling offsets. The 385 cm corner span plus an adjoining 15 cm thickness completes the 400 cm grid. Preserve the current material assignments, vertex paint and authored UVs; do not impose a new kit-wide collision policy from a single sample.

Use the owner's installed Super Duper Batch Exporter preset and verify current settings, rather than reviving earlier trial exports. See [BLENDER_WORKFLOW.md](BLENDER_WORKFLOW.md). Preserve intentional tileable-density exceptions. Check the actual imported piece and collision in Unreal before declaring a new delivery verified.
